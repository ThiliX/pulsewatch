# Code of Conduct

PulseWatch follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone taking part in this project (in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space) is
expected to follow it.

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming. Assume good intent, and critique ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, sexualised language or imagery, or personal attacks.
- Don't publish other people's private information without their permission.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and take responsibility when you get something wrong.

The full text at the link above is the binding version.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through their GitHub
profile ([@ThiliX](https://github.com/ThiliX)). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or
permanently ban anyone who does so, following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
